# How to say technical words that misaki, Kokoro's English phonemizer, skips or gets wrong.
#
#   word = how to say it
#
# The right side is either a respelling in words misaki knows (other words from this list
# may appear in it), or phonemes between slashes in misaki's US set, stress mark before the
# vowel: `ˈ` primary, `ˌ` secondary; `A I O W Y` are the vowels of "day my go now boy".
# Write the vowel of "father" as `ɑː`: American voices drop the `ː`, British ones keep it
# (a plain `ɑ` is the vowel of "lot" for them).
# Words match whole and ignore case; plurals and possessives (`enums`, `Redis's`) follow.
# Entries under `[exact case]` match only as written, for acronyms that are also words.
#
# Most entries are words misaki has no pronunciation for: without espeak-ng it would skip
# them silently. Check a change with `uv run --with mlx-whisper scripts/pronunciation_check.py`.
